ATHLETICS MANAGER — ROAD TO BETA
PHASE 2: CORE CAREER FLOW AUDIT

Purpose
Audit the actual week-to-week career journey on top of the signed-off Phase 1 stability baseline.

Primary flow
New career -> onboarding -> Home -> week advance -> inbox/actions -> selection/registration -> competition -> live event -> results -> return -> next week.

Audit focus
- no invisible blockers
- no duplicate required actions
- no dead-end screens
- no incorrect or stale attention states
- no broken navigation handoffs
- no competition flow that skips, loops or strands the player
- no save/reload breakage at critical handoff points
- no feature expansion unless required to repair a proven flow defect

Exit
Core career flow is understandable and technically reliable, with Phase 1 critical QA still green.
